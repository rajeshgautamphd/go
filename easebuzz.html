⚠️ Key Finding

Easebuzz has raised $30M from Bessemer Venture Partners and 8i Ventures to build a B2B payments and vertical SaaS platform for Indian SMBs, yet has zero AI discoverability. With 0 G2 reviews and 0 Reddit mentions, Easebuzz is invisible in the AI-driven search channels where Indian businesses now research payment gateways — a market where competitors Razorpay and Cashfree have built massive content moats and strong AI-search presence. This gap is particularly acute given Easebuzz's vertical SaaS differentiation, which is not being surfaced to AI-powered decision-makers.

🔍 AI Search Query Analysis

"best payment gateway India 2026"WEAK
This is the single most valuable query in Easebuzz's market — and they are completely absent from AI-generated answers. Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU, and Stripe dominate with thousands of reviews, comparison blog posts, and technical documentation. Indian SMBs increasingly rely on AI search tools (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) to evaluate payment gateways, and Easebuzz's absence from these results means they are losing discovery at the most critical decision point.
"B2B payment solutions for SMBs"WEAK
B2B SMB payments is Easebuzz's core market positioning, yet the company does not appear in AI search results for this category-defining query. Competitors like Razorpay have invested heavily in SMB-focused content marketing, developer documentation, and community engagement — building the citation footprint AI models use to rank solutions. Easebuzz's vertical SaaS angle (industry-specific payment workflows) is a powerful differentiator that remains completely hidden from AI search.
"UPI payment integration platform"WEAK
UPI integration is a table-stakes capability for any Indian payment platform and a key search query for developers and businesses. AI search results feature Razorpay, Cashfree, and PhonePe's developer docs — not Easebuzz, despite their UPI and QR capabilities. The company's technical documentation is not sufficiently indexed or cited to register in AI models. Developer-focused content (API docs, integration guides, SDK comparisons) would directly improve visibility for this query family.
"vertical SaaS payments India"WEAK
This query perfectly captures Easebuzz's unique value proposition — vertical SaaS payment solutions for specific industries like education, logistics, and retail. Yet the company is invisible. No competitor owns this niche query either, creating a significant first-mover opportunity. Easebuzz should build vertical-specific landing pages (e.g., "Payment Solutions for Education Businesses") with structured data to own this query before competitors recognize the gap.
"payment aggregator software comparison"WEAK
Comparison queries represent high-intent purchase signals, but Easebuzz is absent from AI-generated payment aggregator comparisons. Razorpay vs. Cashfree comparisons dominate, with PayU and Stripe India also appearing. Easebuzz needs to publish structured comparison content — both on their own domain and through third-party fintech publications — to participate in the comparison dialogue that AI models synthesize into recommendations.
