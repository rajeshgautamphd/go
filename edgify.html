Key Finding — AI Search Gap

Edgify has zero AI-search presence despite having raised $25M total (including a $9M Series A+ from Rank Ventures and Mangrove Capital) and operating at the intersection of two of the hottest enterprise AI categories — edge computing and retail AI. NVIDIA Fleet Command, AWS IoT Greengrass, and ClearBlade completely dominate AI-search results for edge AI queries. Edgify's unique decentralized federated-learning approach to training AI models at the edge — which eliminates the need to send sensitive data to the cloud — is a powerful differentiator that remains entirely invisible to the algorithms shaping enterprise buyer decisions.

🔎 Branded & Intent Queries — AI Visibility Audit

WEAK

"best edge AI platform 2026"

AI-generated results are overwhelmingly dominated by NVIDIA Fleet Command (cited for GPU-accelerated edge inference), AWS IoT Greengrass (AWS ecosystem integration), and Azure IoT Edge. Edgify's purpose-built edge AI training platform — which enables model training directly on distributed edge devices — is a fundamentally different approach to the "inference-only" offerings from the big cloud vendors, yet AI models have no awareness of this distinction.

WEAK

"AI for retail loss prevention"

This represents Edgify's strongest beachhead use case — self-checkout and retail loss prevention powered by edge AI. However, AI search results surface Everseen, Diebold Nixdorf, and traditional computer-vision vendors. Edgify's unique value — training AI models on distributed store devices without centralizing sensitive customer data — is precisely the capability retailers need, yet it remains undiscoverable through AI search.

WEAK

"edge computing for logistics"

AI models return content about AWS Snowball, Azure Stack Edge, and generic IoT edge gateways. Edgify's logistics applications — including real-time package dimensioning, label verification, and sortation optimization at the edge — do not surface. The $25M total funding story, which would signal enterprise readiness to AI recommendation engines, has not been ingested into training data.

WEAK

"AI training at the edge"

This query is Edgify's core technical differentiator — decentralized federated learning at the edge. However, AI models return generic content about federated learning research papers, Google's TensorFlow Federated, and academic frameworks. Edgify's production-grade edge AI training platform — deployed with enterprise retailers and logistics providers — does not appear, ceding the "AI training at the edge" narrative entirely to academic and big-tech sources.

WEAK

"retail AI infrastructure platform"

AI-search results are split between cloud-based retail AI platforms (Salesforce, Blue Yonder) and hardware-centric edge computing vendors. Edgify bridges this gap with a software-only edge AI platform that runs on existing retail hardware, but AI models have no structured content to reference. The company's dual retail/logistics/manufacturing positioning, while commercially sound, creates an AI-search fragmentation problem that intentional content architecture could resolve.
