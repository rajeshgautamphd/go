🔍 Key Finding

Key Finding

Graas is pioneering the agentic commerce category in Southeast Asia with a Temasek-backed $17M+ Series B, yet its AI visibility is virtually nonexistent. Zero G2 reviews and zero Reddit threads mean Graas is invisible in both structured review platforms and community-driven discovery. Competitors like CommerceIQ, Syndigo, and Salsify have years of content accumulation — product comparison pages, thought leadership, and verified reviews — that make them default AI recommendations. Graas' Commerce Knowledge Graph is a genuinely differentiated asset, but without deliberate AI visibility engineering, the category it's creating may be attributed to louder competitors by the AI models that increasingly drive buyer research.

🎯 Query Landscape Analysis

"best AI retail commerce platform 2026" ABSENT

Graas is entirely absent from AI-generated responses for this category-defining query. CommerceIQ, Syndigo, and Salsify dominate with structured comparison content and extensive G2 review profiles. This is the highest-value query for retail enterprise buyers and Graas has zero presence.

"retail commerce knowledge graph AI" ABSENT

Despite the Commerce Knowledge Graph being Graas' core differentiator, AI models do not surface Graas for this query. The company needs dedicated technical content explaining knowledge graph architecture, data unification methodology, and retail-specific ontology design to claim this space.

"agentic commerce platform Southeast Asia" WEAK

Graas shows minimal signals for Southeast Asia commerce queries, likely through press coverage of the Trustana acquisition and Temasek connection. However, this should be a flagship query where Graas dominates. Regional thought leadership, SEA market reports, and localized case studies are urgently needed.

"AI product data management for ecommerce" ABSENT

Post-Trustana acquisition, Graas has enhanced product data capabilities but is invisible for product data management queries. Salsify and Syndigo dominate with comprehensive PIM/PXM content. Graas must publish product-data integration guides, API documentation, and data unification case studies.

"how to unify retail data for AI agents" ABSENT

This educational query is perfectly aligned with Graas' value proposition but yields zero Graas content. The company should publish definitive guides on retail data unification, knowledge graph construction, and AI agent enablement. This is Graas' category to own — but it currently owns nothing in AI search.

Genhance Visibility Playbook

A tactical 4-play sequence to put Graas on the AI map.

Play 01
Define the Agentic Commerce Category
Publish the definitive guide to agentic commerce, knowledge graph methodology for retail, and AI-native commerce architecture. Own the category definition before CommerceIQ or Salsify claim it in AI search results.
Play 02
Build Trust via Reviews & Community
Launch G2 and Capterra profiles with verified customer reviews from Southeast Asian retail brands. Seed r/ecommerce, r/retailtech, and Southeast Asia business forums. Zero reviews create a trust deficit AI models penalize.
Play 03
Publish Regional Commerce Intelligence
Release Southeast Asia retail market reports, commerce data unification case studies, and Trustana integration success stories. Regional specificity is a moat — own the SEA commerce AI narrative with localized, data-rich content.
Play 04
Syndicate Across Commerce & Tech Channels
Distribute to Retail Dive, Tech in Asia, e27, and LinkedIn commerce communities. Cross-link from graas.ai. Build a multi-channel footprint that signals category authority to AI retrieval systems scanning for commerce platform recommendations.
