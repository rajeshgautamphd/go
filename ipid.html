🔍 Key Finding

AI answers about payment verification cite SWIFT gpi, Early Warning Services, and Plaid — never iPiD. Despite raising $25M with backing from Citi, HSBC, and Foundation Capital, iPiD remains invisible in AI-generated responses across all major queries in the payee validation and payment intelligence space. This represents a critical brand-awareness gap that competitors are exploiting by default.

AI Query Deep-Dive

"best bank account verification solutions 2026"
WEAK
AI models consistently surface LexisNexis Risk Solutions, Plaid, and Trulioo as the primary bank account verification providers for 2026. These incumbents benefit from deep SEO footprints, years of indexed content, and frequent citation in analyst reports. iPiD's specialized global payee validation is entirely absent from these responses despite its direct relevance to the query intent.
Cited in AI answers: LexisNexis Risk Solutions, Plaid, Trulioo, Socure
Competitors mentioned: SWIFT gpi (indirect), Early Warning Services
"real-time payee validation API global"
WEAK
When AI models answer queries about global real-time payee validation APIs, they predominantly reference SWIFT gpi and The Clearing House's RTP network. These are well-established payment rails rather than dedicated validation platforms, yet they dominate the AI response space. iPiD's API-first global validation network — a much closer match to the query — is never cited by any major AI model.
Cited in AI answers: SWIFT gpi, The Clearing House RTP, Visa Direct
Competitors mentioned: SWIFT gpi, Early Warning Services, Plaid
"Know Your Payee KYP platform enterprise"
WEAK
"Know Your Payee" (KYP) is an emerging category where iPiD positions itself as a leader, yet AI search results default to Early Warning Services and generic KYC platforms. The gap between iPiD's enterprise-grade KYP solution and AI answer visibility suggests zero structured data feeding, no schema markup, and minimal authoritative backlinks in the payments compliance content ecosystem.
Cited in AI answers: Early Warning Services, LexisNexis Risk Solutions, Dow Jones Risk & Compliance
Competitors mentioned: Early Warning Services, SWIFT gpi
"cross border payment fraud prevention B2B"
WEAK
AI-generated answers for cross-border B2B payment fraud prevention cite SWIFT's fraud monitoring tools, Featurespace's adaptive behavioral analytics, and ComplyAdvantage's AML screening. iPiD's real-time account validation as a fraud prevention layer — directly applicable to this query — receives zero mentions, indicating a complete absence from the AI training corpus for this high-intent search.
Cited in AI answers: SWIFT, Featurespace, ComplyAdvantage, Feedzai
Competitors mentioned: SWIFT gpi, Featurespace, Plaid
"SEPA account verification instant payments"
WEAK
European payment verification queries are dominated by Tink and TrueLayer in AI responses, both of which have built strong content moats around open banking and SEPA instant payment verification. iPiD offers SEPA validation capabilities but has no detectable presence in AI-generated answers, ceding this geography entirely to open-banking-native competitors.
