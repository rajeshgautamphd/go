⚠ KEY FINDING

Melius, backed by CRV and General Catalyst, is building an AI agent lab for creative work — but AI search engines don't know about it. Queries for "AI ad creative generator" and "AI content creation platform" return Canva, Adobe Firefly, and Runway consistently. Melius appears in 1 of 5 category queries and has zero Reddit/community discussion. With 10 G2 reviews vs Canva's thousands, the AI-generated answer to "best AI creative tools" will not include Melius until its UGC presence grows.

Category Query Analysis

"AI ad creative generator for marketing teams" WEAK

SERP dominated by Creatify AI, Canva, and Adobe Express. Melius does not appear. No Reddit threads cited — AI answer built from vendor pages.

"AI content creation platform for brands" WEAK

Canva, Runway, and Adobe Firefly dominate both AI Overviews and organic results. Melius not found in top 10. Community discussion threads about Canva/Adobe appear in related searches but not about Melius.

"creative operating system for ad campaigns" WEAK

Very sparse SERP — mostly agency blog posts. Zero UGC sources. Melius's "creative OS" positioning has no AI-indexed presence for this query despite it being their core value proposition.

"AI video and image ad maker for performance marketing" PRESENT

Melius appears in editorial lists alongside Creatify AI. 10 G2 reviews give some presence, but competitor Creatify has 200+ reviews — Melius is invisible in AI-generated comparison summaries.

"automated ad campaign production tool enterprise" WEAK

Enterprise-focused query returns Adobe Firefly and custom agency solutions. Melius's agentic approach to creative production is not indexed. No UGC content about enterprise creative automation.
