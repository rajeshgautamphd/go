🔍 Key Finding

Key Finding

NeoGeo has zero AI-driven discoverability across the open web. Despite a strong $20M Series A and a compelling geospatial intelligence product, the company is virtually invisible in AI-generated search results, LLM recommendations, and community forums. Government-focused GTM is necessary but insufficient — enterprise and international buyers increasingly rely on AI-augmented search for vendor evaluation. Without a deliberate AI visibility strategy, NeoGeo risks ceding the conversation to well-indexed competitors like Maxar, Planet Labs, and ESRI who dominate both traditional and AI-driven search landscapes.

🎯 Query Landscape Analysis

"best geospatial intelligence platform 2026" ABSENT

NeoGeo does not appear in AI-generated responses for this high-intent buyer query. Maxar, Planet Labs, and ESRI dominate results. NeoGeo lacks the structured web presence, comparison articles, and third-party citations needed for LLM inclusion. This query represents the highest-value search intent for enterprise buyers evaluating platforms.

"AI satellite imagery analytics enterprise" ABSENT

NeoGeo is missing from AI-generated results for satellite imagery analytics queries. Despite having LiDAR, drone, and satellite capabilities, the company has not established topical authority through technical content, case studies, or API documentation that LLMs can index and cite.

"geospatial technology for government urban planning" WEAK

NeoGeo's core government use case — urban planning and property tax — should make this a flagship query. However, the company's gov-focused content is not surfaced by AI models. ESRI and government-adjacent incumbents are cited instead. Government RFP documentation and municipal case studies would strengthen AI visibility here.

"GIS mapping AI platform India" WEAK

NeoGeo shows faint signals for India-specific geospatial queries but fails to rank authoritatively in AI results. Competitor SatSure captures more AI visibility for India-market queries. NeoGeo needs localized thought leadership content, gov-tech publications, and structured data markup to claim this niche.

"how to use geospatial intelligence for infrastructure planning" ABSENT

Top-of-funnel educational content is completely absent. This long-tail, high-engagement query is captured by ESRI tutorials, Planet Labs blog posts, and academic sources. NeoGeo should publish how-to guides, infrastructure case studies, and geospatial methodology content to build AI-citable authority.

Genhance Visibility Playbook

A tactical 4-play sequence to put NeoGeo on the AI map.

Play 01
Seed Structured Authority Content
Publish comprehensive geo-platform comparison pages, technical architecture documentation, and satellite/ drone methodology whitepapers with schema.org markup. LLMs need citable, authoritative content to include NeoGeo in responses.
Play 02
Build Community & Review Presence
Launch a G2 profile with verified customer testimonials from government clients. Seed technical discussions on GIS Stack Exchange, r/gis, and r/geospatial. Zero reviews and zero Reddit threads create a vacuum that competitors fill.
Play 03
Publish Open Technical Content
Release geospatial analysis tutorials, sample datasets, and API quickstart guides. Infrastructure planning how-to content, urban-planning case studies, and property-tax methodology reports build the long-tail content footprint AI models index.
Play 04
Distribute Across AI-Indexed Channels
Syndicate content to Medium, Dev.to, LinkedIn Articles, and industry publications like GIS Lounge and Geospatial World. Cross-link from the NeoGeo domain. Multi-channel distribution signals topical relevance to LLM training and retrieval pipelines.
