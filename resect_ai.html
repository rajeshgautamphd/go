⚠ Key Finding

Resect AI just emerged from stealth with $25M to build an accountability layer for enterprise LLMs — a genuinely novel category. But as a newly public company, it has zero G2 reviews, zero Reddit presence, and minimal search visibility. The "LLM hallucination detection" problem is one of the most urgent pain points in enterprise AI adoption heading into 2027. Arize AI, WhyLabs, and Galileo already own observability queries. Resect must differentiate between "observability" (monitoring) and "accountability" (real-time hallucination interception) and own that distinction in search before competitors fold it into their own messaging.

Search Query Analysis
"best AI model monitoring platform 2026" Q1
ABSENT

Arize AI, WhyLabs, and Fiddler AI dominate this query with comparison content, G2 category pages, and technical blogs. Resect does not appear — and for good reason: Resect is not "monitoring," it's "accountability." But buyers don't search for "LLM accountability platform" yet. "AI observability vs accountability platforms" Q3
ABSENT

No one owns this query — because no one has drawn this distinction yet. Resect could publish the definitive piece defining the difference between observing AI behavior and actively modifying it. This is a category-creation play: a pillar page that defines the "accountability layer" concept, ranks for a unique query, and serves as a linkable asset for every future mention of the category.

WEAK
