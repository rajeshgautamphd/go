Key Finding — AI Search Gap

Ryft is virtually invisible to AI-powered search. Despite raising the largest UK payments Series B of 2026 (£20M from Gresham House Ventures), the platform returns zero AI-generated mentions on any of its five highest-value purchase-intent queries. Stripe Connect, Adyen, and Checkout.com completely dominate the AI-search landscape — Ryft's innovative marketplace payout engine and UK-regulated status remain unknown to the algorithms shaping buyer decisions.
