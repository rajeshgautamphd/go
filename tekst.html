⚠️ Critical UGC Gap Detected
Tekst has raised €11.5M to build agentic process automation and enterprise AI process intelligence — entering one of the most review-intensive software categories in B2B. The process automation and process mining market is dominated by UiPath, Celonis, Automation Anywhere, and Microsoft Power Automate, each with thousands of G2 reviews, active community forums, and deep Reddit presence. Tekst has zero G2 reviews and zero Reddit mentions, making it completely invisible to enterprise buyers using AI-powered search to evaluate "agentic process automation" and "AI process intelligence" tools. Without a UGC footprint, Tekst cannot participate in the AI-mediated buyer journey — even if its technology is superior.

🔍 Category Query Performance

WEAK
"best agentic process automation 2026"
This is an emerging category query where Tekst should be a contender — yet AI search engines return UiPath (with its "AI-powered automation" repositioning) and Automation Anywhere (with "agentic automation" messaging) almost exclusively. Celonis also surfaces for process intelligence-adjacent queries. All three competitors have deep G2 review moats that AI search engines treat as trust signals, effectively locking out Tekst despite its agentic-first architecture.
WEAK
"enterprise AI process intelligence"
Celonis owns "process intelligence" in AI search results, backed by its market-defining position, thousands of G2 reviews, and extensive analyst recognition. Microsoft Power Automate also surfaces due to its massive install base and Microsoft ecosystem integration. Tekst's "Enterprise AI Process Intelligence" positioning directly competes with Celonis, but without third-party validation, AI search engines cannot differentiate Tekst's claims from vaporware.
WEAK
"business process automation AI tools"
UiPath and Automation Anywhere capture the vast majority of AI search real estate for BPA queries, each with thousands of G2 reviews across multiple categories (RPA, Process Mining, Intelligent Automation). Microsoft Power Automate dominates the SMB and Microsoft-ecosystem segment. Tekst does not appear because it has zero indexed content connecting its platform to "business process automation" — a category where review volume is the primary ranking signal.
WEAK
"agentic automation platform enterprise"
Automation Anywhere leads this query with its "agentic automation" product positioning, while UiPath's "AI agents for automation" messaging also surfaces. Both have invested heavily in content marketing around "agentic" terminology. Tekst — despite being purpose-built as an agentic process automation platform — has no indexed content competing for this term, ceding an emerging category to incumbents with larger content engines.
WEAK
"best process mining AI tools"
Celonis owns this query completely in both traditional and AI search results. Its process mining heritage, academic origins, and massive G2 presence make it the default answer for any "process mining" query. UiPath also surfaces through its Process Mining acquisition. Tekst's process intelligence angle is blocked from discovery because it has no reviews, no comparison content, and no community discussions validating its process mining and intelligence capabilities.
