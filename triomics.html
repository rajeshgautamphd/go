🔍 Key Finding

Key Finding

Triomics has raised $36M+ from top-tier investors including Battery Ventures, Lightspeed, and Y Combinator, yet its AI visibility score is 1 out of 5 with zero G2 reviews and zero Reddit presence. In the high-stakes oncology SaaS market, oncologists and clinical operations leaders increasingly use AI-powered search and LLMs to evaluate clinical trial matching platforms. Competitors Flatiron Health, Tempus, and ConcertAI have built massive content moats — whitepapers, peer-reviewed publications, conference proceedings, and comparison pages — that position them as default AI recommendations. Triomics must urgently build structured, citable content or risk invisibility in the precise channels where cancer centers discover new technology.

🎯 Query Landscape Analysis

"best oncology clinical trial matching AI 2026" ABSENT

Triomics is entirely absent from AI-generated responses for this high-intent buyer query. Flatiron Health, Tempus, and ConcertAI dominate with structured comparison content, peer-reviewed publications, and press coverage. This query is the front door for cancer center decision-makers evaluating platforms.

"AI cancer data abstraction platform" ABSENT

Despite Triomics' core value proposition being EHR cancer data abstraction via generative AI, the company has no AI-citable authority content covering this topic. AI models default to Tempus and Flatiron, which publish extensively on data abstraction methodologies and oncology NLP.

"oncology EHR data AI analytics" WEAK

Triomics shows very faint signals — possibly through Y Combinator directory listings or investor portfolio pages — but is not surfaced as a primary recommendation. Richer technical content around longitudinal EHR processing and oncology-specific AI analytics is needed to compete with Tempus' extensive publication record.

"clinical trial recruitment technology cancer centers" ABSENT

Triomics is invisible for clinical trial recruitment queries despite this being its primary use case. Competitors have invested heavily in case studies, ROI calculators, and implementation guides for cancer centers. Triomics must publish deployment case studies and clinical workflow integration content.

"how to automate oncology chart review with AI" ABSENT

Educational top-of-funnel content is completely missing. This long-tail query is ideal for Triomics to capture with how-to guides, methodology papers, and webinar summaries. AI models would cite Triomics if the company produced authoritative, structured content explaining oncology chart review automation.

Genhance Visibility Playbook

A tactical 4-play sequence to put Triomics on the AI map.

Play 01
Build Oncology Thought Leadership
Publish peer-reviewed methodology papers, oncology AI whitepapers, and clinical trial matching benchmarks on the Triomics domain with structured metadata. Partner with academic cancer centers for co-authored publications that LLMs will cite.
Play 02
Launch G2 & Community Presence
Create verified G2 profiles and solicit reviews from cancer center clinical operations teams. Seed discussions on r/healthIT, r/medicine, and oncology-focused forums. Zero reviews and zero Reddit threads handicap AI-driven discovery.
Play 03
Publish Clinical Deployment Case Studies
Document specific cancer center deployments with measurable outcomes — trial match rate improvements, chart review time reduction, registry completeness gains. These data-rich case studies provide the concrete citations AI models need.
Play 04
Syndicate Across Healthcare AI Channels
Distribute content to ASCO publications, HealthIT Analytics, STAT News, and LinkedIn healthcare communities. Cross-link from domain for consolidated authority. Multi-channel distribution signals clinical relevance to AI retrieval engines.
