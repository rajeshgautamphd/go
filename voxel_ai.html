🔶 KEY FINDING: Competitors Dominate AI Search Citations
Despite $71M in total funding and a compelling AI-powered workplace safety vision, Voxel AI is nearly invisible in AI-generated search results. AI Overviews and AI-cited sources consistently reference competitors — particularly Intenseye, Protex AI, and viAct — while Voxel appears in only 2 of 5 high-intent buyer queries. With just 7 G2 reviews and only 1 Reddit mention, Voxel lacks the user-generated content signals that modern AI search engines rely on for citation and trust. This represents a significant competitive blind spot in AI-driven discovery channels.

🔍 AI Search Query Analysis

"best workplace safety AI software 2026" ✅ PRESENT
Voxel appears in some editorial roundups and listicles, but AI Overviews cite Intenseye and Protex AI far more prominently, often as the primary recommendations. Voxel is mentioned only in passing, if at all, in the AI-generated summaries that buyers see first.
"computer vision for warehouse safety" ⚠️ WEAK
AI-generated answers consistently cite Protex AI, viAct, and Coram AI as the leading solutions for warehouse computer vision safety. Voxel is entirely absent from the sources that AI search engines reference, despite being purpose-built for this exact use case.
"AI safety monitoring platform reviews" ⚠️ WEAK
With only 7 G2 reviews compared to Intenseye's 50+, Voxel has minimal review-site presence. Reddit threads discussing AI safety monitoring cite competitors exclusively. AI search heavily weights review volume and UGC signals — areas where Voxel is critically underrepresented.
"how to prevent workplace injuries with AI" ✅ PRESENT
Voxel appears in some editorial roundups about AI injury prevention. However, AI Overviews pull content primarily from workers' compensation publications and industry safety journals — not from Voxel's own content or product pages, limiting the depth of its presence in AI-cited results.
"best EHS software with AI 2026" ⚠️ WEAK
AI answers for EHS (Environmental, Health & Safety) software queries overwhelmingly cite traditional suites like Intelex and Cority. Voxel is not mentioned in any AI-cited source for this category, despite its AI-native approach that could differentiate it from legacy EHS platforms.
