🔍 Key Finding

Key Finding

Wellinks has a clinically compelling product — virtual COPD care with remote monitoring — backed by a strategic UMass Memorial Health investment, yet its AI visibility score is 1 out of 5 with zero G2 reviews and zero Reddit threads. In the rapidly consolidating digital health market, competitors like Hinge Health and Omada Health have built enormous content moats spanning clinical evidence libraries, peer-reviewed publications, and community engagement. Pulmonary and respiratory care buyers — pulmonologists, health system administrators, and payer clinical teams — are increasingly influenced by AI-driven search when evaluating virtual care platforms. Wellinks' focused respiratory niche is a genuine advantage, but without an AI visibility strategy, the broader digital MSK players will capture the cardiopulmonary conversation by default.

🎯 Query Landscape Analysis

"best virtual pulmonary rehabilitation platform 2026" ABSENT

Wellinks is completely absent from AI-generated responses for this high-intent query. This should be Wellinks' flagship search term given its focused COPD and respiratory care positioning. Hinge Health and Omada Health, despite being MSK-focused, capture broader digital health queries through their massive content engines.

"remote patient monitoring COPD AI" ABSENT

Wellinks' core value proposition — remote patient monitoring for COPD — should make this query a natural win. However, AI models surface generic RPM vendors and academic medical center programs instead. Dedicated RPM-for-COPD content, clinical protocols, and monitoring methodology documentation are missing.

"digital cardiopulmonary care platform enterprise" WEAK

Wellinks shows faint signals — possibly through the UMass Memorial Health partnership announcement or health system press coverage — but is not surfaced as a primary recommendation. The cardiopulmonary niche is relatively uncrowded in AI search, presenting a significant first-mover advantage if Wellinks acts quickly.

"virtual chronic disease management healthcare" ABSENT

Wellinks is invisible for broad chronic disease management queries. While this is a competitive space dominated by Omada, Livongo, and Hinge Health, Wellinks' pulmonary specialization could differentiate it — but only if it publishes content connecting COPD care to broader chronic disease management outcomes.

"how to deliver COPD care remotely with technology" ABSENT

Educational top-of-funnel content is absent. This query is ideal for Wellinks to capture with how-to guides, clinical workflow documentation, and remote spirometry integration content. Pulmonologists and care teams searching for practical COPD telehealth guidance find nothing from Wellinks.

Genhance Visibility Playbook

A tactical 4-play sequence to put Wellinks on the AI map.

Play 01
Own the Virtual Pulmonary Rehab Category
Publish the definitive guide to virtual pulmonary rehabilitation — clinical evidence, RPM protocols, COPD care pathways, and integration workflows. The cardiopulmonary digital health niche is contested by MSK players; Wellinks should own it in AI search before they do.
Play 02
Build Clinical Community & G2 Presence
Launch verified G2 and Capterra profiles with reviews from pulmonologists and care coordinators. Seed respiratory care discussions on r/medicine, r/respiratorytherapy, and clinician forums. Zero reviews in a clinical market undermines AI credibility scores.
Play 03
Publish Clinical Outcomes & Partnership Content
Document the UMass Memorial Health deployment with measurable outcomes — COPD readmission reduction, patient adherence rates, RPM utilization data. Clinical evidence with specific metrics is the highest-signal content for AI models in healthcare.
Play 04
Syndicate Across Digital Health Channels
Distribute to Fierce Healthcare, MobiHealthNews, Telehealth and Medicine Today, ATS (American Thoracic Society) channels, and LinkedIn healthcare communities. Cross-link from wellinks.com. Multi-channel distribution builds the clinical authority footprint AI models prioritize.
