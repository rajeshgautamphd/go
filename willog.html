⚠️ Key Finding

Willog has progressed to Series B-2 with backing from seven investors, signaling strong product-market fit for its AIoT-powered supply chain visibility platform — yet the company has zero AI-search discoverability. With 0 G2 reviews, 0 Reddit mentions, and no presence in supply chain technology analyst reports, Willog is invisible to logistics and supply chain leaders who are increasingly using AI search tools to evaluate real-time visibility platforms. This is a critical gap in a market where competitors FourKites and Project44 have built dominant AI-search moats through aggressive content marketing, analyst relations, and community engagement.

🔍 AI Search Query Analysis

"best supply chain visibility platform 2026"WEAK
This is the highest-value query in Willog's category, and the company is completely absent from AI-generated answers. FourKites, Project44, and Tive dominate AI search results with Gartner Magic Quadrant citations, thousands of G2 reviews, and extensive case study libraries. Willog's AIoT differentiation — combining IoT sensor data with AI-powered risk intelligence — is not visible to buyers evaluating platforms because there are zero third-party signals for AI models to reference.
"real-time logistics tracking software"WEAK
Real-time tracking is a core Willog capability, but the company does not appear in AI search results for this query. The market is dominated by FourKites and Project44, who have built massive content libraries around real-time visibility use cases, customer success stories, and technical whitepapers. Willog's hardware+software AIoT approach is a genuine differentiator, but without indexed case studies and technical documentation, AI models cannot surface it.
"AIoT supply chain monitoring"WEAK
AIoT (AI + IoT) supply chain monitoring is Willog's exact category positioning, making this the most aligned query in their market. Yet even for this niche, AI search results are sparse — and do not include Willog. This represents both a problem and an opportunity: the query is not yet owned by any competitor, meaning Willog could establish first-mover AI visibility by creating definitive content on AIoT supply chain monitoring and earning citations from logistics and IoT publications.
"cold chain monitoring solution"WEAK
Cold chain monitoring is a high-value vertical within supply chain visibility — particularly for pharmaceuticals, food, and biologics — and Willog's sensor technology is well-suited for this use case. AI search results feature Roambee, Tive, and Controlant, but not Willog. The company's cold chain capabilities need dedicated landing pages, pharmaceutical/logistics case studies, and industry conference speaking engagements to build the authoritative signal AI models require.
"supply chain risk management platform"WEAK
Supply chain risk management is a rapidly growing category accelerated by post-pandemic awareness of supply chain fragility. Willog's risk intelligence capabilities — analyzing IoT sensor data for predictive risk signals — directly address this need, yet AI search results feature Everstream Analytics, Resilinc, and risk modules from FourKites/Project44. Willog needs to position its AIoT data as a unique risk intelligence input and build content around predictive supply chain risk use cases.
